BFF signup admission is phone-bound, MAC-signed, short-lived and consumed once with delivery reservation; IP/device admission precedes the provider call, and GoTrue validates the one-use CAPTCHA before invoking the signed hook. The token alone is not represented as a separate CAPTCHA proof. Native metadata forwarding was verified on actual GoTrue. User-editable roles/verified flags are ignored.
